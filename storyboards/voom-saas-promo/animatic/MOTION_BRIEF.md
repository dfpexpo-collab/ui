# VOOM promo v02: motion brief (art direction)

**Feel:** premium SaaS launch film. Calm confidence, never busy. One hero move per beat; everything else supports.
References: the three SaaS templates (glass cards, tilted screens, big kinetic words) in VOOM colours only.

## Rules
- **Camera never stops.** Every scene has one eased move: push-in, pan or 3D drift (sine in-out, scene length). The scene settles before the cut.
- **Backgrounds:** the frame stays put; only the light inside moves (glows drift and breathe).
- **Entrances vary by role:** cards rise with blur, icon tiles and status dots pop, side panels slide in from their own side, devices tilt up in 3D. Stagger 0.1 s.
- **Small squares appear as pixels** (stepped, randomised build), never all at once.
- **Cursor:** travels on a curved path, sweeps over grids and buttons on the way, and they grow toward it (proximity), then clicks with a ring.
- **Text:** the settings saved in Text Studio (echo + blur headlines, typewriter fields, count-up numbers).
- **Transitions by meaning:** dark→light and light→dark are a gradient wipe; the two "ball" (dot-grid) transitions are F03→F04 and F09→F10 (from the AI button click); dark→dark beats use a whip with motion blur; F14 pushes through the screen into the footage.
- **Logo:** mask reveal with a light streak, no distortion.
