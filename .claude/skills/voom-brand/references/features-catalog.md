# Voom product features catalog

Source: *New Features* product sheets (22 pages). Use this to pick the right feature, headline, one-liner and hero visual for any piece. Wording below is the brand's own where quoted. Each entry lists **what to show** so layouts and storyboards stay accurate.

Product frame: **AI-powered real estate digital platform**, web-based, works on laptop, desktop, tablet, phone. URLs of live projects follow `<project>.voom.cc` (e.g. `badr-2.voom.cc/master-plan`).
