# Voom brand foundations

## Who Voom is

- **Name:** VOOM. Tagline / descriptor: *Proptech & Digital Twin Innovation*.
- **Symbol:** a small rocket-like swoosh that rises off the final **M** of the wordmark. It means speed, launch, lift-off ("voom!"). The second **O** is drawn as a wide rounded rectangle, like a screen or visor: the digital window into a project.
- **Positioning line (used on covers, web hero, social cover):** *Redefining how off-plan real estate is presented.*
- **Description:** Voom is the first AI-powered PropTech platform transforming the way off-plan real estate projects are showcased, sold, and managed.
- **What it does:** turns paper-based, brochure-and-spreadsheet sales into a smooth, fully digital experience. Developers get interactive masterplans, detailed 3D exterior models, rotatable unit views, realistic floor plans, interior walkthroughs, smart availability and filtering, and CRM sync. All web-based, also available offline.
- **Handle / web:** `@voomproptech` (X, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook), `www.voomproptech.com`. Per-project sites live on `*.voom.cc`.
- **Market context:** Saudi Arabia and the wider GCC and Egypt (Riyadh HQ per stationery; clients include Maya, Pasha Developers, First Avenue, K Developments, Palmier, Al Dawlia, Alramz, Alomar). Arabic is a primary language.

## Audience

1. **Real estate developers / marketing & sales directors** who sell off-plan and want to impress and convert (primary buyer).
2. **Sales teams / brokers** who present on tablets at launches, exhibitions and on site.
3. **End buyers** who use the experience: they want to see their exact unit, view, floor plan and price quickly.

Speak to the developer's outcome (more clarity, faster decisions, better sales conversations), not to the technology.

## Personality (from the brand book sliders)

| Axis | Voom sits | Meaning for creative |
|---|---|---|
| Modern <-> Traditional | **Strongly modern** | Clean layouts, glass UI, 3D, motion. No ornament, no clip-art. |
| Playful <-> Serious | **Leans serious** | Credible, premium, precise. Wit allowed in hooks (see social posts) but visuals stay controlled. |
| Accessible <-> Luxury | **Leans accessible** | Premium feel without elitism. Plain words, clear benefits. |
| Trendy <-> Timeless | **Strongly timeless** | Avoid fad effects. Deep blue gradients, strong type, generous space age well. |

Brand words: **innovative, precise, fast, immersive, confident, clear.**

## Tone of voice

- **Short and concrete.** "Explore a detailed floor plan for any unit with ease." One idea per sentence.
- **Benefit first, feature second.** Lead with what the client can do or see, then name the feature.
- **Confident, not hyped.** No "revolutionary!!!" stacks. Claims are specific (16K zoom, real-time CRM sync, works offline).
- **Sentence case** headlines. No ALL CAPS paragraphs. All caps only for tiny labels or a hero keyword (as in "EXPLORE OUR PROJECTS").
- **Verbs we like:** explore, present, sell, see, walk through, filter, sync, launch, redefine.
- **Words to avoid:** cheap, hack, magic, crazy, best ever, guaranteed returns, anything promising investment performance or legal outcomes.
- **Add the disclaimer** "Images are for illustrative purposes only and may differ from reality" when showing renders as product (the product UI itself carries it).

### Arabic voice
- Conversational Gulf/Saudi-friendly Arabic is used in social (see `ideas-and-copy.md`): punchy questions, contrast pairs ("old way vs Voom"), a repeated hashtag device.
- Headlines in social are often stretched with kashida for rhythm. For body and UI use normal letter-joining. Keep it readable first.
- Mirror layouts for RTL. Numerals: follow the platform convention of the audience (Western digits are used in the examples). Do not mix digit systems inside one design.

## Messaging pillars

1. **Present** - make a project that is not built yet feel real (3D masterplan, exterior, interior tours, day/night).
2. **Sell** - connect interest to action (smart search, filtration, info card, Register Interest / Callback).
3. **Manage** - live availability synced with the CRM (available, reserved, sold, unlaunched).
4. **Anywhere** - web-based on any device, plus offline version for exhibitions and low-connectivity sites.
5. **Intelligent** - AI-powered search that suggests closest matches; a digital twin of the project.

- 16K resolution zoom on the masterplan.
- Integrates with any CRM (Zoho, Salesforce, HubSpot and more are shown on the sheet).
- Works offline.
- Delivered projects shown in the portfolio: Palencia (K Developments), Al-hada (First Avenue), Stax (Pasha Developers), Badr 2 and Sama-Alorod (Maya), Taya Industrial City (TIC), plus Palmier "Vert", Alomar "Dama" projects, Al Dawlia "Regents Square", Alramz "Jadeel Al-rimal". Client logos belong to those clients: show them only in portfolio contexts.

## What not to say or show

- No investment-return promises, price predictions or legal claims.
- Don't name competitors.
- Don't show other brands' logos as if they were partners. For real co-branding use the partnership rule in `visual-system.md`.
- Don't use the client's UI colors as Voom colors.
