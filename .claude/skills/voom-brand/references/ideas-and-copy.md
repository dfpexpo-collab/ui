## Idea generator (use the structure, vary the topic)

Every idea gets: **Hook** (<= 8 words), **Insight** (the developer pain), **Visual** (what we see), **Copy** (AR + EN), **Feature proof** (from the catalog), **CTA**, **Format**.

### Angles

1. **Paper vs digital:** brochures, price lists, Excel sheets, site visits vs one link.
2. **Proof before the build:** "See your unit before it exists" (interior tour, balcony view, floor plan).
3. **Speed to decision:** client filters by budget and area in seconds; closest matches suggested.
4. **Always current:** units sync with the CRM; sold and reserved update live; sales team and client see the same truth.
5. **Present anywhere:** exhibition stand, client's majlis, a field office with no internet (offline).
6. **Location sells:** interactive map shows landmarks and travel time; strategic location story.
7. **Day and night:** the project at golden hour and lit at night; emotional selling.
8. **Behind the scenes:** from 3D render to digital twin, how a project goes live in weeks (do not state a duration unless supplied).
9. **Portfolio spotlight:** one project per post (Stax tower, Al-hada, Badr 2...). Client logo + project name + 1 feature.
10. **Myth busting:** "Digital twin isn't just a 3D video. It sells."
11. **Numbers:** 16K zoom, X units synced, N projects delivered (use only verified numbers).
12. **Event teaser / recap:** summit, exhibition, launch day.

## Copy patterns

- **Feature caption (EN):** `<Feature name>` + one sentence: `<what the client can do>` + `<why it matters>`. Example: *Smart Search Engine: filters units based on your criteria, shows availability and suggests the closest matches.*
- **Benefit headline:** verb + outcome, <= 6 words. Example: *Sell with full visibility.*
- **Contrast pair:** `<Old pain>?` / `<Voom answer>.`
- **Social caption:** line 1 = hook, line 2-3 = proof, line 4 = CTA, then hashtags: `#Voom #PropTech #DigitalTwin #OffPlan #RealEstate` (+ Arabic `#كل_شي_تغيّر_مع_VOOM` for AR posts). Max 5 hashtags.
- **Event post:** what + where + when + what we show + CTA. Include the event badge only with permission.

## Naming conventions

Product name is always **Voom** in running text, **VOOM** only as the logo. Feature names in Title Case as in the catalog. "off-plan" lowercase with a hyphen. "PropTech" with capital P and T in text; "Proptech" appears in the logo tagline as drawn, do not retype it.
