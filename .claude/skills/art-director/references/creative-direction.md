# Creative direction playbook

## Moves that make work feel premium
- **One guide object** travels the whole piece and causes events (a spark lands → a word appears).
- **Hero word treatment**: kashida growth that pushes its neighbours, weight shift, a highlight bar, a colour swap; everything else mask-rises quietly.
- **Match cuts on shared objects** (tower → tower in the dashboard), **zoom-through** into a bright point for "deeper" ideas, **text-led cuts** where the last word stays.
- **Depth**: background 0.3×, bokeh 0.5×, imagery 1×, foreground light 1.2×. Copy stays planted.
- **Living holds**: sheen once across a card, rings pinging on taps, glow breathing on the beat. Never a frozen page.
- **Beat grid**: cuts and landings on beats; hero hits on downbeats with a ≤ 7 % scale punch.
- **Restraint**: one idea per scene; if two things compete, cut one.

## Things that make work look cheap (call them out)
- Everything fades/slides in the same way, then the page sits still.
- The same dissolve between every scene.
- Text with blur trails, echoes or bouncing that the brief didn't ask for.
- Cut-outs with halos; plates as visible rectangles; empty frame edges.
- Synth "bleeps" on every word; music with no drop.
- Over-designed: too many effects in one frame, no focal point.

## Writing a direction (example)
> **Idea:** "Your whole real-estate business, run from one place" — the viewer should feel control and calm power.
> **Tone:** confident, glossy, precise — like an Apple product film in deep Voom blue.
> **Hero moments:** S1 the hand reaches through fluted glass and the question resolves on "واحد؟" as its kashida stretches; S5 10X lands on the downbeat with a sub hit …
> **Motion language:** expo-out entries (0.6-0.9 s), exits at half; camera pushes only on imagery; copy never moves after landing.
> **Type:** Thmanyah; one hero word per scene with kashida growth; supporting lines mask-rise per line.
> **Transitions:** zoom-through 1→2, dissolve 2→3, push 3→4, pixel 5→6, match 7→8, zoom-out 9→10.
> **Sound:** real SFX library; ~1 cue per event; drop on the cover reveal.
> **Do-nots:** no echo text, no keyed text, no empty frame edges.

## Giving feedback that gets executed
- Timestamp + scene + element + problem + fix, in one line.
- Show, don't describe: attach the crop or the side-by-side.
- Rank: blockers first, then polish, then ideas.
- Keep what works — say it, so it isn't "fixed" away.
